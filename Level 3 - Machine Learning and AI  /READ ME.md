## Level 3 | Advanced Machine Learning and Introductory AI

This section delves deeper into the realm of machine learning and introduces fundamental concepts of Artificial Intelligence. The coursework demonstrates my understanding of:

* **Supervised Learning (Advanced):** Building upon the basics, this includes in-depth knowledge and practical application of specific supervised learning algorithms:
    * **Logistic Regression:** For binary classification problems.
    * **Decision Trees:** Tree-based models for both classification and regression.
    * **Random Forests:** Ensemble learning techniques using multiple decision trees.
* **Unsupervised Learning:** Exploring patterns and structures in unlabeled data, specifically focusing on:
    * **K-Means Clustering:** An algorithm for partitioning data into distinct groups.
* **Natural Language Processing (NLP):** Introduction to the techniques and applications of processing and understanding human language.

The **Capstone Project** for this level involved an **analysis of existing AI projects in the real world**. This project required me to research and evaluate various applications of AI, demonstrating an understanding of current trends, challenges, and potential impacts of AI technologies.
